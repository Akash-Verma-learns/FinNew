Apple Inc. — Equity Research Report
Analyst: Sample Research Team
Date: November 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) reported total net sales of $391.035 billion for fiscal year 2024
(ended September 28, 2024), representing a modest 2% increase year-over-year from $383.3
billion in FY2023. Services revenue reached an all-time high of $96.2 billion, growing 13%
annually and now representing nearly 25% of total revenue.

The company achieved a gross margin of 46.2% in FY2024, up from 44.1% in the prior year,
driven by the higher-margin Services segment. Net income was $93.7 billion, yielding diluted
earnings per share of $6.11.

VALUATION MODEL

Our DCF model applies a WACC of 8.5% and a terminal growth rate of 3.0%, yielding an
intrinsic value estimate of $215 per share. We apply an exit P/E multiple of 28x to our
FY2026 EPS estimate of $7.50, arriving at a price target of $210.

BALANCE SHEET HIGHLIGHTS

Apple ended FY2024 with $156.8 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities.
Long-term debt stood at $85.8 billion. The company repurchased $94.9 billion in shares during
the fiscal year, reducing its share count by approximately 3%.

ACCOUNTING POLICY NOTE

Apple uses the first-in, first-out (FIFO) method for inventory valuation. The company
depreciates property, plant, and equipment using the straight-line method over estimated
useful lives of 1 to 30 years.

FORWARD GUIDANCE

For Q1 FY2025, Apple guided total revenue of $124–$125 billion, implying approximately
5% year-over-year growth. Management expects Services gross margin to remain above 74%.

RECOMMENDATION

We rate Apple BUY with a 12-month price target of $210. The stock offers a compelling
combination of durable free cash flow generation ($108.8 billion in FY2024 operating cash
flow), an expanding services flywheel, and disciplined capital return programme.
